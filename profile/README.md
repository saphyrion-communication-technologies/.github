# SAPHYRION Communication Technologies

Saphyrion is a privately owned research and development company that specializes
in the design and development of space-qualified integrated circuits as well as
instruments for GNSS data analysis. For manufacturing a fabless model is used:
a wide choice of external foundries ensures Saphyrion's fast reaction to market
needs. The target customers are the aerospace and professional GNSS industry.

Established in February 2009, Saphyrion is located in Bioggio (near Lugano),
southern Switzerland.
